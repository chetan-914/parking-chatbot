title: How to book a parking space
topic: booking_process
---
You can reserve a space by chatting with this assistant. The steps are:

1. Tell the assistant the day and the start and end time you want to park, for example tomorrow from 3 pm to 5 pm.
2. The assistant checks whether a space is free for that period. If none is free, it suggests nearby times.
3. Give your first name, last name and car number.
4. The assistant shows a summary with your details, the period and the total price, and asks you to confirm.
5. Reply yes to confirm. You receive a booking reference. Keep it, because you may need it if you contact support.

Booking rules: a reservation must start and end on the same day. Start and end times must fall on the hour or the half hour. The shortest reservation is one hour and the longest is twelve hours. You can book up to 14 days ahead but not in the past, and the whole period must fall within the car park's opening hours.

Your car number should be an Indian registration number, for example TS09AB1234 or 22BH1234AA. Spaces and hyphens are fine, because the assistant tidies them up.

You can change your mind at any point before you confirm. Just say so, and nothing is booked. Booking in the chat does not charge you, because payment is made when you leave. You cannot change a confirmed reservation in the chat; to change the time, cancel it and book again.
