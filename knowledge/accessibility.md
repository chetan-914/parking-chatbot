title: Accessibility and assistance
topic: facilities
---
CityPark Central is designed to be step-free from the pedestrian door to every level. The lobby has two lifts that serve all levels. The lift buttons have Braille markings, and the lifts announce each floor aloud. Ramps with handrails connect the lobby to the pavement, and the payment kiosk can be used from a wheelchair.

Two accessible spaces are located in Zone A, next to the lift. They have extra-wide bays and a marked access aisle. They are for drivers and passengers who hold a valid disability parking permit, and the permit must be displayed. These spaces are not assigned through the chat reservation, so please contact the car park team before your visit and ask them to arrange one for you.

There is an accessible toilet in the lobby. Assistance dogs are welcome everywhere in the car park.

Staff are happy to help with luggage, wheelchairs or directions to the lifts. If you would like help on arrival, mention it when you contact the team or tell the attendant at the entrance.

If you notice a lift out of service, a blocked ramp or an accessible bay in use by someone without a permit, please report it to the attendant or call the support number so that it can be fixed.
