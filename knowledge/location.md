CityPark Central is at 42 Example Road, in the central business district of Sample City, postal code 000000. It stands on the corner of Example Road and Market Street, directly opposite Example Metro Station and next to Sample Mall.

The vehicle entrance is on Market Street, on the east side of the building, and is marked with a large blue P sign. The vehicle exit leads onto Example Road. Pedestrians use a separate door beside the vehicle entrance, which opens into a lobby with the lifts and the payment kiosk.

Landmarks that help you find it: Example Metro Station is across the road, about two minutes on foot. Sample Mall adjoins the car park and shares its north wall. City Library is one block to the west.

The car park is in a busy area, and traffic is heaviest when the mall is crowded and at the end of the working day. If you use a navigation app, search for CityPark Central or the street address, and choose the Market Street entrance. The Example Road side is exit only, and cars cannot enter from it.
