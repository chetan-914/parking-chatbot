topic: facilities
---
CityPark Central is monitored by CCTV cameras that cover the entrance, the exit, the aisles, the lobby and the stairwells. A number plate camera records vehicles as they enter and leave. The car park is well lit on every level, and the barriers are controlled by the car park system.

The on-site team patrols the levels, and an attendant is available at the lobby counter. Emergency call buttons are installed on each level and in the lifts, and fire exits and extinguishers are clearly marked. In an emergency, follow the green exit signs and the instructions of the staff.

Vehicles are parked at the owner's risk. Please lock your car, take your valuables with you and do not leave bags or devices in view. The car park is not responsible for items left in a vehicle. If you notice damage, a break-in or anything suspicious, tell the attendant immediately and do not leave the car park before you have reported it.

Camera recordings are kept for 30 days and are shared only with the police or with the authorities, as required by law.

Your reservation details, such as your name and car number, are stored securely and separately from this assistant. The assistant cannot see them again after you book, and it cannot show anyone else's reservation to you or yours to anyone else.

Questions this answers: Is the car park safe? Are there security cameras? What if my car is damaged? Is my data private? Who do I tell about an incident?
