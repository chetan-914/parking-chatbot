topic: facilities
---
CityPark Central has four electric vehicle charging points in Zone B, close to the lift on the lower level. Each point has a Type 2 AC socket and charges one vehicle at a time. Bring your own Type 2 charging cable, because cables are not provided.

Charging points work on a first come, first served basis. They cannot be reserved through this chat, and a normal parking reservation does not assign you a charging bay. If you need to charge, tell the attendant when you arrive and ask whether a point is free. Do not park in a charging bay unless you are plugged in, because other drivers may be waiting.

Charging is billed separately from parking. The amount depends on the electricity used and is shown on the charger display and at the payment kiosk. You settle both parking and charging when you leave.

When your car has finished charging, please move it or unplug it promptly so that the point is free for the next driver. Staff may move a vehicle that stays in a charging bay without charging.

If a charging point shows a fault or does not start, report it to the attendant at the lobby counter or call the support number. Do not unplug or reset equipment yourself.
