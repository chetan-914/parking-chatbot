You do not pay when you book in this chat. Payment is made when you leave the car park, at the payment kiosk in the lobby or at the staffed counter. The amount for a reservation is the total price that the assistant showed you before you confirmed it.

Accepted payment methods:
- UPI: scan the QR code on the kiosk screen with any UPI app.
- Debit and credit cards: Visa, Mastercard and RuPay.
- Cash: accepted at the staffed counter. The counter can give change.

Cheques and international wallets are not accepted. A printed receipt is available at the kiosk, and the counter can provide a receipt on request.

Please keep your payment details private. This assistant will never ask for a card number, a UPI PIN, a bank account number or any other payment detail. If you type one by mistake, a privacy filter removes it from the conversation, and you will see a warning. Never share such details in the chat.

Electric vehicle charging is billed separately from parking and is paid at the same time, when you leave.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, speak to the attendant at the counter or contact support with your booking reference and car number.
