topic: general
---
CityPark Central is a covered, multi-level car park in the city centre. It has 20 parking spaces arranged in two zones, Zone A and Zone B. It is meant for shoppers, office visitors, event guests and travellers who need a safe place to leave a car for part of a day or a whole day.

Visitors can reserve a space in advance through this chat assistant, or simply arrive and park if a space is free. A reservation guarantees a space for the chosen period, so there is no need to circle around looking for one. Reservations are made for a single day, and every reservation starts and ends on the hour or the half hour.

The car park is run by an on-site team and is monitored by cameras. It suits standard passenger cars and small vans within the size limits described in the vehicle rules. It also has electric vehicle charging points, accessible spaces and lifts between the levels.

This assistant can help with the location and directions, how booking works, the cancellation policy, the rules for vehicles, payment methods, security, accessibility and how to reach a person at the car park. For current prices, opening hours and how many spaces are free at a particular time, ask the assistant directly. Those details change, so the assistant looks them up live instead of quoting a fixed answer.

Questions this answers: What is CityPark Central? How big is the car park? What can this assistant help me with? Who is the car park for? Is it covered?
