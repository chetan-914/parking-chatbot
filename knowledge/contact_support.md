title: Contacting the car park team
topic: general
---
If you need a person instead of the assistant, you can reach the CityPark Central team in three ways. By phone: +00 000 000 0000. By email: support@citypark-example.com. In person: visit the staffed counter in the lobby, next to the payment kiosk.

Contact the team when you want to cancel a reservation, arrange an accessible space, ask about a payment or a receipt, report damage or a security problem, report a faulty charging point or lift, make a complaint, or ask about anything this assistant cannot answer.

To help the team find your booking quickly, have these ready: your booking reference, your car number and the date of your reservation. They will use these details to confirm that the booking is yours. Never share payment card details or passwords with the team by email.

Lost property: if you lose something in the car park, report it at the counter or by email. Found items are kept for 30 days.

Email is answered within one working day. For anything urgent while you are in the car park, use an emergency call button on any level or speak to the attendant at the counter.

This chat assistant can answer questions about the car park and take a reservation. It cannot cancel reservations, refund payments or look up another person's details.

Questions this answers: How do I contact someone? What is the support phone number? Where can I report lost property? Who do I speak to about a complaint? Is there a human I can talk to?
