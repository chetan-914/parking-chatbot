title: Cancellation and no-show policy
topic: policy
---
You can cancel a reservation at any time before its start time. There is no cancellation fee, because nothing is charged when you book. You pay only when you leave the car park.

To cancel you need two things: your booking reference and your car number. They must match. The booking assistant in this chat cannot cancel a reservation yet, so please contact the car park team by phone or email, or speak to the attendant at the lobby counter, and give them both details. Once a reservation is cancelled, the space becomes available to other visitors again.

A reservation that has already started cannot be cancelled online. Speak to the attendant if your plans change while you are parked.

If you do not arrive, the space stays reserved for your whole period and cannot be given to anyone else. Cancel before the start time to release it. There is no penalty for a no-show, but please cancel so that other visitors can use the space.

A confirmed reservation cannot be edited. To change the day or the time, cancel the old reservation and make a new one. Check first that a space is free at the new time, because availability changes during the day.

If the car park has to cancel your reservation for a reason such as maintenance work, the team will tell you and help you find another time.
