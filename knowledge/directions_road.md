These directions lead to the vehicle entrance on Market Street.

Coming from the north on Ring Road: take exit 5 towards City Centre and stay on Central Avenue for about two kilometres. At the traffic light after City Library, turn right onto Market Street. The car park entrance is on your right after about 150 metres, just past the main gate of Sample Mall.

Coming from the south on Airport Road: follow the signs for City Centre until the flyover at Station Junction. Take the left service lane under the flyover and turn left onto Market Street. Example Metro Station will be on your right, and the car park entrance is on your left a short way after it.

Coming from the west through the old town: drive along Heritage Street until you reach City Library, then turn left onto Market Street. The entrance is on the right after about 150 metres.

Do not try to enter from Example Road. That side is the exit lane only.

At the entrance, a camera reads your number plate and the barrier opens for visitors who hold a reservation. Follow the blue signs and the attendant's directions to your space. A height-limit bar hangs just inside the entrance, so check the vehicle rules before you drive in with a tall vehicle or a roof box.
