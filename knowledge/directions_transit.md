title: Public transport, taxis and walking
topic: location
---
By metro: take the Blue Line to Example Metro Station and leave by Gate 2. Cross Market Street at the signalled crossing in front of the gate. The car park lobby is on the other side, about two minutes on foot. Many visitors leave their car here and continue by metro, which works well with a reservation of a few hours.

By bus: routes 12, 45 and 78 stop at the Sample Mall bus stop, which is directly in front of the car park's pedestrian door.

By taxi or auto-rickshaw: there is a drop-off and pick-up bay on Example Road, next to the pedestrian door. Please do not stop in the Market Street entrance lane, because it blocks cars waiting to enter.

On foot: from City Library it is a five-minute walk along Market Street. The pavement is level and well lit. The pedestrian door opens into the lobby, where you will find the lifts and the payment kiosk.

By bicycle or two-wheeler: there is no bicycle or motorcycle parking inside this car park. The car park accepts cars and small vans only.
