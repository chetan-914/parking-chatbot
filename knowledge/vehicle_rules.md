---
Accepted vehicles: passenger cars, SUVs and small vans that fit within these limits: height up to 2.1 metres, length up to 5.2 metres, width up to 2.0 metres and weight up to 2.5 tonnes. A height-limit bar at the entrance will touch anything taller. Please remember to include roof boxes, bicycle racks and antennas when you check your height.

Not accepted: trucks, buses, trailers, caravans and motorcycles or scooters. There is no two-wheeler parking.

Rules inside the car park:
- Drive slowly. The speed limit is 10 kilometres per hour.
- Park only inside a marked bay, in the space you were directed to, and keep clear of aisles, ramps, lift doors and fire exits.
- Switch off the engine when parked and do not leave it idling.
- Do not store fuel cans, gas cylinders or other hazardous goods in the vehicle.
- Do not wash or repair vehicles, and do not drain fluids.
- Use your space only during your reserved period, and drive out before the car park closes.
- Do not park in a charging bay unless you are charging an electric vehicle, and do not use an accessible bay without a valid permit.

Vehicles that break these rules may be asked to move, and the car park may refuse entry to vehicles that do not fit the size limits.
